# 🎤 FoldScout — Pitch Notes (60-second demo)

## Hook (10s)
> "AlphaFold predicted 200 million protein structures. But every researcher still opens PyMOL, manually colors by pLDDT, and writes their own first-pass review by hand."

## What is FoldScout (15s)
> "FoldScout is an AI research agent for structural biology. Paste a UniProt or PDB ID, and in 30 seconds you get a 3D viewer with confidence coloring, a per-residue pLDDT plot, and an expert-level structural report written by Gemini."

## Live demo (25s)
1. Click **🔴 TP53** preset
2. Watch the structure load and spin, colored by pLDDT
3. Point out: "Blue = trust this. Orange = disordered, ignore."
4. Read one line from the Gemini report aloud

## Why it matters (10s)
> "This collapses a 15-minute manual workflow — open PyMOL, color by B-factor, scroll PubMed, write notes — into 30 seconds. It's the first triage step every structural biologist will run on every new structure they encounter."
